How it works

Paper in. Finished PDF out.

Three steps, and every one of them runs on your phone.

Fig 01 · Capture

Point the camera. Scannera does the rest.

It finds the page borders, straightens the angle you actually shot it at, and lifts the shadow your hand was casting. Keep going and every page lands in the same document.

Edge detection and perspective correction

Page borders are found instantly and the skew is corrected, so a photo taken at an angle comes out square. You can adjust the crop by hand whenever the automatic guess is not what you wanted.

Shadow removal and enhancement

Uneven lighting and hand shadows are removed. Five filters sharpen contrast and readability: Original, Grayscale, B&W, Magic Color and Auto Enhance.

Multi-page batch scanning

Capture page after page in one session, review them together at the end, and export the lot as a single PDF. Pages per scan are capped on the free tier and unlimited on Pro.

Dedicated capture modes

Modes for documents, agreements, ID cards, driving licences, passports and business cards, so the crop and enhancement suit what you are actually scanning.

Fig 02 · Read

Your scan becomes text you can use.

On-device OCR reads the page back as selectable text you can copy, share and search. The recognition model ships inside the app, so the page never goes anywhere to be read.

Six scripts, recognised offline

Latin-script languages along with Hindi and Marathi, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. It is not a universal, every-language engine, and results depend on how clear the scan is.

Search the words inside your documents

Full-text search runs across the text recognised in your scans, not just file and folder names, so you can find a page by something written on it.

Copy, paste, share

Recognised text comes back as plain text, ready for an email, a note or a message. The scanned page is untouched, so you keep the PDF as well as the words.

Fig 03 · Finish

Sign it, stamp it, send it.

Mark up the page, drop in your signature, add a watermark and export. Import PDFs you already have and edit those the same way. The whole edit happens locally.

Annotate and fill

Draw, highlight, add text, shapes, arrows, dates, images and sticky notes directly on a page, and fill out PDF forms.

Signatures

Draw a signature, type one, or photograph one on white paper and have the background removed. Saved signatures live in a library and can be placed on any document.

Custom watermarks

Stamp your own text on exported PDFs and shared images before they leave your phone. Reuse the ones you use often.

Import and export

Bring in existing PDFs and images, re-export after editing, or share a single page as an image.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Are my scans uploaded anywhere?

No. Scanning, editing, filtering, text recognition and PDF export all happen on your device. The one exception is Google Drive backup, which is off by default and goes to your own account. Anonymous crash reports and usage analytics are sent, never your documents.

Does Scannera work offline?

Yes, in airplane mode. A connection is only needed to buy Pro or to sync Drive backups.

Which languages does the OCR read?

Latin-script languages, plus Hindi and Marathi, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Accuracy depends on how clear the scan is, so proofread anything important.

Can I search the text inside my scans?

Yes. Search runs across the recognised text as well as document and folder names.

Can I sign documents?

Yes. Draw a signature, type one, or photograph one on white paper and have the background removed. Saved signatures can be placed on any document. Signing is a Pro feature.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-up, no email, no profile. A Google sign-in is only needed if you turn on Drive backup.

Is there a subscription?

No. Scannera is free to use, and Pro is a single one-time purchase with lifetime access, restorable on any device signed in to the same Google Play account.

What can it scan?

Contracts, receipts, invoices, whiteboards, lecture notes, business cards, warranties and identity documents, with dedicated capture modes for each.
