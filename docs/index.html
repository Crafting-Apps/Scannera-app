FAQ

Questions, answered

Are my scans uploaded anywhere?

No. Scanning, editing, filtering and PDF export all happen on your device. The only exception is Google Drive backup, which is off by default — when you turn it on, files go to your own Google account and nowhere else. Anonymous crash reports and usage analytics are sent, never your documents.

Do I need an account to use Scannera?

Never. There's no sign-up, no email and no profile. Install it and start scanning immediately.

Does it work without internet?

Yes — every core feature works fully offline, including scanning, annotation, watermarking and export. An internet connection is only needed to purchase Pro or to sync Drive backups.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Scannera Pro is a single one-time purchase with lifetime access, restorable on any device signed in to the same Google Play account.

What can it scan?

Multi-page contracts and reports, receipts and invoices, whiteboards and lecture notes, IDs and passports, business cards, warranty certificates — plus QR codes and every standard barcode format.

Can I import PDFs I already have?

Yes. Import existing images or PDFs from your device, then organise, annotate, watermark and re-export them just like a fresh scan.
