The short version

Three questions worth asking before you install a scanner

Where does the page go?

A scanner that enhances, reads or stores your page on a server has to receive the page first. Passports, tax forms and contracts are exactly the documents you would rather not hand to a third party. Scannera processes them on your phone's own hardware.

Who are you to the app?

Most of the big scanners want an account before you can scan: an email, a profile, a file history tied to your identity. Scannera has no sign-up at all. A Google sign-in appears only if you choose to turn on Drive backup to your own account.

What does it cost in five years?

A yearly plan is not a price, it is a direction of travel. At around US$60 a year, five years of a scanner subscription runs to roughly US$300. Scannera Pro is US$9.99, paid once, restorable on any device on the same Google Play account.

Being fair about it

When one of the others is the better choice

No app wins on every axis, and we would rather you installed the right one than ours.

You need iPhone, desktop or web as well. Scannera is Android only. CamScanner, Adobe Scan and Genius Scan all run across platforms and keep a copy of everything in sync, which is genuinely useful if you scan on a phone and file on a laptop.

You need OCR in a language we do not bundle. An on-device model is a fixed size, so ours covers Latin scripts plus Hindi, Marathi, Chinese, Japanese and Korean. Cloud OCR has no such ceiling and reads far more languages.

You are buying for a team. Shared folders, admin controls, seat management and audit trails are what the business tiers of the subscription apps are for. Scannera is built for one person and their own paperwork.

You want your scans in a provider's cloud by default. Some people do, and it is a reasonable preference. Scannera's backup is optional, off by default, and goes to your own Google Drive rather than to us.

FAQ

Questions people ask when switching

Is Scannera a good CamScanner alternative?

If what you want is scanning, OCR, PDF editing and signing without a subscription or an account, yes. If you rely on CamScanner's cloud sync across phone, desktop and web, or on its team features, it still does things Scannera does not.

Can I move my existing scans across?

Export your documents as PDFs or images from your current app, then import them into Scannera from your device. Imported PDFs can be annotated, signed and watermarked like anything you scan here.

Why is Scannera cheaper than the others?

Because there are no servers to pay for. Processing runs on your phone, so there is no per-scan cloud cost to recover from you every year. That is the trade: no cloud bill, and no cloud.

Is Pro really one payment?

Yes. One purchase through Google Play with lifetime access, restorable on any device signed in to the same Play account. There is no renewal and no second tier above it.

What replaced Microsoft Lens?

Microsoft retired Lens in early 2026 and points users to the scanner inside the OneDrive app, which requires a Microsoft account and saves scans to OneDrive. Scannera needs no account and keeps files on the device, with Google Drive backup available if you want it.

Does Scannera show ads?

The free tier is limited rather than ad-funded: page and document caps and a small export watermark, which Pro removes. Your documents are never used to target anything.
