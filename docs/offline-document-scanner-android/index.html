How it works

What makes Scannera an offline document scanner?

“Offline” only means something if the work actually happens on your phone. In Scannera, it does.

Every step of a normal scanning session runs on your Android device, using the phone's own camera and processor. With the app installed you can put the phone in airplane mode and still complete a full document from capture to exported PDF:

  • Document capture. The camera preview and shutter, including the dedicated modes for documents, agreements, ID cards, licences, passports and business cards.
  • Edge detection and perspective correction. Page borders are found and the skew is straightened locally.
  • Image filtering. Original, Grayscale, Black & White, Magic Color and Auto Enhance are applied on the device.
  • OCR. Text recognition uses a model bundled with the app, so no page is sent away to be read.
  • Annotation and signatures. Drawing, highlighting, text, shapes, notes and signature placement.
  • Watermarking. Your own text is stamped onto exports before they leave the app.
  • PDF creation and export. Single and multi-page PDFs are assembled and written locally, then shared wherever you choose.

A connection is only needed for two things: buying the one-time Pro upgrade through Google Play, and syncing Google Drive backup if you have turned it on. Scannera also sends anonymous crash reports and usage analytics, never your documents. The privacy policy sets out exactly what that covers.

The case for it

Why use an offline document scanner?

No internet required

Aeroplanes, basements, site offices, classrooms, trains and patchy rural coverage are all ordinary places to need a scan. Scannera does not wait for a signal to finish the job.

Better privacy

Tax forms, medical letters, contracts and IDs are processed on your own hardware rather than being handed to someone else's scanning service to read.

No account required

There is no sign-up, no email and no profile. Install the app and the scanner is ready. Nothing is gated behind registration.

Fast local processing

Pages are not uploaded and downloaded again to be enhanced or read, so a batch of scans finishes at the speed of your phone rather than your connection.

Fewer places for data to sit

Files that were never uploaded do not need to be tracked down and deleted later. What stays on your device stays under your control.

Cloud backup still available

Offline processing does not mean no backup. Turn on Google Drive backup and finished PDFs copy to your own Google account, on Wi-Fi only if you prefer.

Text recognition

Offline OCR on Android

Scannera can recognise the text in a scanned page using an OCR model that ships inside the app. Because the model is on the device, recognition works with no connection and the page itself is never sent anywhere to be read.

The bundled model recognises Latin-script text, so it suits documents written in English and other languages using the Latin alphabet. It is not a universal, every-language engine, and like any OCR its results depend on the quality of the scan: a flat, well-lit, in-focus page reads far better than a creased receipt photographed in the dark. Expect to proofread the output rather than trust it blindly.

Once a page has been recognised, the extracted text can be copied and shared as plain text, so it can go straight into an email, a note or a document. One thing worth knowing: the in-app search matches document and folder names, not the recognised text inside your scans. The OCR scanner page goes further into how recognition works and where its limits are.

Privacy

Your documents stay on your device

Scanning, editing and filtering, text recognition, annotation and signing, and PDF generation all happen locally on your Android phone. There is no account, no profile and no upload step in a normal scanning session. The file you export is written on your device and goes only where you send it.

There is one deliberate exception, and it is worth stating plainly. Google Drive backup is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, Scannera signs in to your Google account and copies your scanned PDFs to your Drive automatically, and over Wi-Fi only if you prefer. It asks for access to the files it creates in your Drive, not the rest of your Drive. Nothing goes to a Scannera server; there isn't one for your documents.

Scannera is also not an app that never touches the internet. It uses a connection to verify a Pro purchase through Google Play, to sync Drive backups when you have enabled them, and to send anonymous crash reports and usage analytics that help fix problems across different Android devices. That diagnostic data contains no document content. The Scannera privacy policy spells out each of these in detail, and the private document scanner page covers the architecture behind them.

FAQ

Offline scanning, answered

Does Scannera work without internet?

Yes. Capture, edge detection, filtering, OCR, annotation, signing, watermarking and PDF export all work with no connection, so you can complete a document in airplane mode. A connection is only needed to buy the one-time Pro upgrade through Google Play, to sync Google Drive backup if you have enabled it, and to send anonymous diagnostics.

Can I create PDFs offline?

Yes. Scanned pages are assembled and written into a PDF on your device, including multi-page documents captured in batch mode. Free scans are limited to a set number of pages per batch and Pro removes that limit, but neither the free nor the Pro export needs a connection.

Does Scannera have offline OCR?

Yes. Text recognition uses an OCR model bundled with the app, so it runs on the device with no upload. The bundled model covers Latin-script text, and accuracy depends on how clear the scan is, so proofread anything important. Recognised text can be copied and shared as plain text.

Do I need an account to use Scannera?

No. There is no sign-up, no email and no profile. Install the app and start scanning. A Google sign-in is only involved if you choose to turn on Drive backup.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

Not in normal use. Scanning, editing, OCR and PDF generation all happen on your device. The exception is Google Drive backup, which is off by default; when you enable it, your PDFs are copied to your own Google account and nowhere else, and Scannera can only see the files it creates there. Anonymous crash reports and usage analytics are sent, never your documents. See the privacy policy for the detail.

Can I annotate PDFs offline?

Yes. Drawing, highlighting, text, shapes, arrows, dates, sticky notes and signatures all work without a connection, on scans you have just taken and on PDFs you import from your device.

Is Scannera available for Android?

Yes. Scannera runs on Android 8.0 and later and is available on Google Play. It is free to use, with an optional one-time Pro upgrade and no subscription.
